Medical Disclaimer

Symptom Tracker Pro is not a medical device. The application does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or advice. It functions strictly as a personal digital notebook for manually logging symptoms, food intake, and notes. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding any medical conditions or health decisions.

Data Collection and Usage

All data is stored locally in an offline SQLite database. No cloud syncing or external servers are used. Your data never leaves your device unless you export it via CSV.

Internet Access and Permissions

The app requests network permissions only to open external links (such as this Privacy Policy). No personal information or analytics are transmitted.

Data Control and Deletion

You can view, search, and delete entries directly in the app. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all local data.
